This week in 60 seconds AI-drafted · source-linked

One thing you can still do this week: the Standing Committee on Finance is taking views on the Indian Statistical Institute Bill until Sunday, 28 September — email comfinance@lss.sansad.in. Otherwise the pipeline is in its post-session lull. Criminal law and justice is the only topic genuinely heating up, driven by three actions in July: the anti-paper-leak law passing, a Bill to add Supreme Court judges, and the Bar Council's draft to amend the Advocates Act. Personal finance is steady but busy — a tax-law amendment passed, IRDAI's insurance regulations went live, and the repo rate stayed at 5.25% for the fifth meeting running. Gig work, AI and digital rights recorded no new formal action; the labour-code rules are now in the implementation phase, where PRS stops watching and unions start.
